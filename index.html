Hello, I’m George M. Fragiadakis, a Postdoctoral Researcher and Adjunct Lecturer at the Department of Informatics and Telematics, Harokopio University of Athens (HUA), and an AI Engineer at VML (WPP group) in Athens.

My research is on Human-AI Collaboration (HAIC): how to measure, in a rigorous and domain-agnostic way, whether people and AI systems actually work well together — trust and its calibration, the effort humans spend correcting or overriding AI, adaptability, and usability. Within the Horizon Europe project HumAIne I designed and built the HAIC Benchmark Suite, an evaluation platform and the open-source Python library haic-libs (haic-logging, haic-metrics) that compute collaboration metrics from structured interaction logs. It has been validated in real pilots, including a public-services pilot on smart-city permit applications, and extended with probabilistic surrogate-user simulation for testing collaboration scenarios before deployment.

In the Horizon Europe project CERTAIN I lead Task 4.6 on the energy and e-waste footprint of AI systems and data-space federations, working on energy-aware profiling of AI inference and its integration into AI sandbox environments. I also contribute to European AI standardisation work (CEN/CENELEC JTC 21) and to the NexusForum R&I roadmap.

My PhD research explored the techno-economic analysis of cloud services, applying machine learning to predict cloud pricing and support decision-making in multi-cloud environments. That line continues in work on FinOps — extending cost governance to the design stage of cloud systems — and on decision support for cybersecurity investment optimisation.

Before academia I spent about ten years in industry as a cloud, IoT and DevOps engineer (Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD, observability). Today, at VML, I work on agentic AI systems and their evaluation and observability, which keeps my research close to how AI is actually built and deployed.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Physics from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, with an Erasmus stay at the Niels Bohr Institute (University of Copenhagen), and a Master’s degree in Informatics and Telematics from Harokopio University.

I teach cloud management (CI/CD, Terraform, OpenStack/GCP labs) at undergraduate level and, at MSc level, AI applications (agent fundamentals and HAIC methodologies), introductory Python and AI, and technology economics / FinOps. I also supervise BSc and MSc theses on cloud cost engineering, FinOps and applied AI.
