This line grows out of my PhD on the **techno-economic analysis of cloud services**.

- **Cloud price prediction** with machine learning (e.g. Amazon IaaS) and clustering-based cost comparison across providers
- **Cloud PricingOps** — a decision-support framework for exploring cloud pricing policies across providers
- **Design-time FinOps** — extending the FinOps lifecycle to the pre-deployment stage, so cost is evaluated while a system is being designed, not only after the first bill
- **LLM-as-a-Service economics** — the cost perspective of adopting hosted large language models

The work also feeds my teaching (FinOps, cloud management) and a series of BSc/MSc theses on cloud pricing data pipelines and cost dashboards.

See the [publications](/publications/) page for the related papers.
