A sensor-agnostic, data-driven pipeline that derives **per-device sampling policies** for battery-powered LoRaWAN sensors from their own historical data, so devices sample less when the environment is stable.

Validated offline on 62 indoor-air-quality sensors of the HUA Smart Campus, with projected battery-lifetime extensions of 45–96%.

Presented at SmartAgri & SuSY 2026 (Tirana): *Smart Campus Context-Aware Policies for the Efficient Operation of Battery-Powered IoT Devices* — with A. Villios, G. Kousiouris, E. Filiopoulou and M. Nikolaidou.
