Within the Horizon Europe project [CERTAIN](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101189650) (GA 101189650) I lead **Task 4.6 — e-waste and energy footprint minimisation in AI systems and dataspace federations**.

Main lines of work:

- **Energy-aware profiling of AI inference** — measuring the energy and carbon footprint of models across deployments and precision modes, and combining it with live cloud pricing into a single, operator-weighted sustainability score for decision support
- **Sandbox plugin** — an energy-profiling plugin for AI sandbox environments, so that any imported model/dataset can be assessed for its footprint alongside other trustworthiness checks
- **Federation monitoring** — energy attribution for data-space connector activity and storage-footprint (e-waste proxy) tracking
- **Standardisation** — mapping task metrics to emerging AI sustainability standards

Related publication: *Towards Sustainable AI: Sustainability Metrics for Inference Engines in Different Deployments Focusing on Carbon Footprint and Deployment Cost* (IEEE SMC 2026).
